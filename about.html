WHO WE ARE

Education with purpose.

We are building a school community where every learner is known, supported and challenged to do their best.

OUR STORY

A school community built around possibility.

Excellent Heights Academy is presented as a private-school learning community committed to helping children develop academically, socially and personally. Our approach brings together caring relationships, structured teaching and opportunities to explore the world.

From the first steps in early years to the demands of senior secondary education, we believe each stage should prepare learners for the next with confidence and curiosity.

OUR MISSION

To inspire learning that makes a difference.

To provide a supportive and stimulating education that develops knowledge, character, creativity and a sense of responsibility in every learner.

OUR VISION

Confident learners. Positive impact.

To nurture a generation of thoughtful, capable and compassionate individuals prepared to contribute meaningfully to their communities and the wider world.
