HOW TO APPLY

A simple path to getting started.

  1. 1

    Make an enquiry

    Contact the school to ask about available classes and the admission process.

  2. 2

    Complete the form

    Provide the learner and parent or guardian details in the application form.

  3. 3

    Prepare documents

    Gather the supporting documents listed below for review by the school.

  4. 4

    Await communication

    The school office will advise on any next steps after reviewing the enquiry.

Documents to prepare

  • Recent passport photograph
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Previous school report (where applicable)
  • Transfer or leaving document (where applicable)

Application deadline and available places should be confirmed directly with the school. No real deadline has been supplied for this project.
